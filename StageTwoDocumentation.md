# 🚗 MAZAADY - Car Auction Platform

An **online live auction** platform for buying and selling used cars easily and securely — no need to visit showrooms!  
The platform ensures **trust**, **transparency**, and **a complete digital experience** for all parties.

---

## STAGE 2:

## 📌 Purpose
To create an online auction platform for used cars that replaces traditional in-person auctions, makes the process easier for users, and allows the auction to reach a wider audience.

---

## 📈 Objectives (MVP)
1 - Allow users to list their cars and participate in live online auctions.  
2 - Provide a simple and user-friendly interface to make browsing and bidding easy.  
3 - Enable real-time bidding features so users can join the auction from anywhere.
