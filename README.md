## 💡 Project Idea
A web platform that simplifies the process of selling and buying used cars through **remote live auctions**, while protecting the rights of both the buyer and the seller.

---

## ❗ Problem
There is **no specialized electronic auction** for used cars that saves customers time and effort while maintaining transparency and trust.

---

## ✅ Solution
An **online car auction website** that allows users to buy or sell cars remotely, with verified listings and a trusted transaction process.

---

## 📌 Purpose

To create an online auction platform for used cars that replaces traditional in-person auctions, makes the process easier for users, and allows the auction to reach a wider audience.

## 📈 Objectives (MVP):

1 - Allow users to list their cars and participate in live online auctions.

2 - Provide a simple and user-friendly interface to make browsing and bidding easy.

3 - Enable real-time bidding features so users can join the auction from anywhere.

## ⚙️ Project Details

### 🧍 Seller Process
1. Register on the platform and fill out the required information.  
2. Add the car details, upload documents, and attach a *Mojaz* report.  
3. Wait for admin verification. Once approved, the car is added to the auction list.

### 🧑‍💻 Admin Process
- Review and verify seller details and *Mojaz* report.  
- Approve or reject listings based on provided information.  

### 🏁 Auction Process
1. The auction starts **one week** after the car is listed.  
2. Buyers can browse available cars and join auctions.  
3. To participate, the buyer must:
   - Register and complete their profile  
   - Pay a **deposit of 1,500 SAR** to confirm seriousness  
4. If the buyer doesn’t purchase, they can request a refund of the deposit.  

### 💰 Commission
- Platform commission: **2%** of the total sale price.  
- Deducted from the deposit amount.  
- If the commission exceeds the deposit, the buyer pays the remaining amount.  
- After sale completion, both buyer and seller receive an **email confirmation** with:
  - Sale details  
  - Buyer and seller info  
  - Ownership transfer steps
